rclone-simon privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

rclone-simon is used by one person, Simon Szeto, to back up his own files to his own Google Drive.

  • The app reads files from Simon's own PC and uploads them to Simon's own Google Drive.
  • The Google permission drive.file limits access to the files that the app created.
  • The sign-in key is stored only on Simon's PC. It is not sent to any server other than Google.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared, or sent to any third party.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking is used.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
